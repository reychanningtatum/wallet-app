Mission & Vision

Founded by Reychan Bernaldez, a computer engineer, entrepreneur, and sales executive with a passion for intelligent systems and behavioral finance, Fundly was created to redefine how individuals manage and understand money in everyday life.

Combining expertise in technology, business strategy, and real-world financial behavior, Reychan envisioned a platform that goes beyond traditional budgeting — delivering adaptive financial pacing, real-time spending intelligence, and sustainable financial discipline designed for modern users.
